Which axes get normalized: BatchNorm, LayerNorm, InstanceNorm, GroupNorm, RMSNorm

Every normalization layer subtracts a mean and divides by a standard deviation (RMSNorm skips the mean). They differ only in which numbers share one mean. The block is a batch of activations: N examples, C channels, and the H×W positions flattened into one axis. Click a cell to light up the cells it is normalized with; drag to turn the block.
