A ResNet is an ensemble of paths: 2n routes through n blocks, and the short ones do the work

Every residual block offers the signal two routes: through the block, or around it on the shortcut. Stack n blocks and there are 2n ways through (Veit, Wilber and Belongie 2016). Click a block to see which paths use it; click a path to trace it.
