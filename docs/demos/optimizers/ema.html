EMA and bias correction: the machinery inside Adam

The raw exponential moving average starts at zero no matter what the data is, so it crawls up from underneath early on. Dividing by (1−βt) undoes exactly that.

β 0.90
EMA*t = β·EMA*t−1 + (1−β)·xt, starting at EMA*0=0. Bias-corrected: EMAt = EMA*t / (1−βt). At t=1 the correction factor is 1/(1−β) — large; by t≈20 it is close to 1.
