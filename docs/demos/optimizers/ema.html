EMA and bias correction: three ways to average a stream

Unlike a running average, which averages every point forever, the exponential moving average (EMA) smoothly averages a window of recent points. But the raw EMA starts at zero even if the data starts nonzero, which can dramatically underestimate signals at first. The bias-corrected fixes that instantly, then after the start behaves like a moving EMA average.
