Adam vs. SGD, rescaled: why Adam is scale-invariant

The same tilted bowl as the anisotropy demo, run under SGD (orange) and Adam (blue) at once. Slide K to multiply every gradient by a constant — SGD's steps grow with K; Adam's barely change.

SGD:
z←z−ηKg
Adam:
z←z−ηm/√v
a:1 20 tilt −25°
η 0.120 gradient scale K 1.0×
Scaling g by K scales Adam's m and √v equally, so K cancels in m/√v — SGD has no such cancellation. Drag the 3D surface to rotate it.
