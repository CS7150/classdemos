Adam vs. SGD, rescaled: why Adam is scale-invariant

The same bowl, the same start, run under SGD (orange) and Adam (blue). Slide K to multiply every gradient by a constant — SGD's steps grow with K; Adam's barely change.

learning rate 0.120
gradient scale K 1.00× (log scale, 0.1× to 10×)
SGD: Δv = −η·g. Adam: Δv = −η·m/(√v + ε) with EMAs m≈g, v≈g², so scaling g by K scales m by K and √v by K — the K cancels.
