Mean vs. RMS: the diagnostic behind Adam

A synthetic gradient stream. When it flips sign often, the mean cancels toward zero while the RMS does not — that gap is exactly what Adam watches to decide when to slow down.

drift 0.00 sign-flip freq 1.20 noise 0.40
mean = (1/N)∑gt, RMS = √((1/N)∑gt2), over the last 40 samples shown. Adam's step is proportional to mean/RMS — near 1 when the sign is consistent, near 0 when it flips often.
