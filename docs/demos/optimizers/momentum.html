Momentum: averaging out the noise, before Adam

A smooth bowl with a high-frequency ripple riding on top. Plain gradient descent (orange) gets stuck bouncing inside one ripple; momentum (blue) averages the alternating ripple gradient toward zero and keeps riding the bowl's gradient down.
