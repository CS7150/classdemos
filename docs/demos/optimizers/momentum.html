Momentum: averaging out the noise

A smooth bowl with a high-frequency ripple riding on top, growing steeper the further out you go and vanishing at the optimum. Plain gradient descent (orange) gets stuck bouncing inside one ripple; momentum (blue) averages the alternating ripple gradient toward zero and keeps riding the bowl's gradient down.

ripple amp 0.08 ripple period 0.22 bowl steepness a 0.40
β 0.85 η 0.080
Orange dashed = smooth bowl alone. Solid curve = bowl + ripple, the actual loss. Momentum's k is an EMA of the gradient — a zero-mean alternating ripple gradient averages toward 0, leaving mostly the slow bowl gradient.
