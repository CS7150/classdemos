The tilted distribution: the optimum of reward minus a KL penalty

Fine-tune a model to earn reward without wandering far from where it started: maximize E[r] − β·KL(π‖πref). The answer has a closed form, and it is the reference model multiplied, response by response, by er/β. Here the “responses” are the points of a single line, so the whole distribution fits in one picture.
