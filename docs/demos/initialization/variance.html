Variance, and n·Var(W): why standard init shrinks the signal

Glorot & Bengio note that the standard initialization gives n·Var(W) = 1/3. This page builds up what that means: what variance is, the four rules that make it easy to push through a layer, and how they turn one layer into “multiply the variance by n·Var(W).”
