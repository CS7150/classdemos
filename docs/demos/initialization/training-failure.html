Bad init, watched training: the failure, not just the theory

A real 6-hidden-layer tanh network training with plain gradient descent on two interleaved moons. Pick an init scale and press Train: watch the decision boundary, the loss curve, and each hidden layer's activation std, live.

scale ×Xavier 1.0× depth 6
Full-batch gradient descent, fixed learning rate, width 10, tanh hidden units, sigmoid output. Too-small init: gradients are tiny from step 1 (see the std bars near the bottom), so loss barely moves in the time it takes Xavier to converge. Too-big init: units saturate immediately, the loss curve is flat or jagged, and the boundary stays close to its random start.
