Depth, activation, init: watching saturation compound

A signal spreading through a deep network, one histogram column per layer. Bars are colored the same way as the weight-scale demo: green where a unit still has a useful gradient, red where it's saturated. Watch the red fraction grow — or not — with depth.

depth 12
W is drawn fresh each layer from the chosen init, width 48 throughout (a square network, so forward and backward agree here). Top: activation histograms by layer, red = saturated (|φ′(h)|<0.05, or a dead unit for relu). Bottom: their std by layer (log scale) — shaded band is the range that survives 24 layers without vanishing or blowing up.
